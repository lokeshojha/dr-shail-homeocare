PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters to us.

This page explains, in general terms, how information shared through the Dr. Shail HomeoCare website may be handled.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use this website or submit an appointment request, you may provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment details and information about your health concerns.

Only information necessary for responding to your enquiry or appointment request should be submitted through the relevant forms.

2. How information may be used

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate about appointment requests and coordinate the consultation process.

Information should not be submitted through the website for emergency medical situations.

3. Appointment requests

Submitting an appointment request does not by itself mean that an appointment has been confirmed. The clinic may contact you to confirm availability and consultation details.

4. WhatsApp and external services

This website may provide links to external services such as WhatsApp. When you choose to use an external service, your interaction may also be subject to that service's own privacy policy and terms.

5. Data security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-party links

The website may contain links to external websites or services. Dr. Shail HomeoCare is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

7. Your choices

If you have questions about information you have submitted through this website, you can contact the clinic through the available communication channels.

8. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page.

Important:

This is a general website privacy-policy template and should be reviewed and finalized according to the clinic's actual data practices and applicable legal requirements before the website is launched publicly.
